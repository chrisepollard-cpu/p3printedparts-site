Services

What we make · How you order

For homeowners, hobbyists, home builders, and repair shops. Same careful process for everyone.

  • Knobs and handles Knobs, handles, pulls, and grips for appliances, cabinets, furniture, tools, and machines of any age. We reuse your metal core when possible. Replacements are sold as “fits [model],” never as factory-branded parts.
  • Fit samples We get a correct fit or match on a sample before any larger run. Every customer starts here.
  • Custom and one-off parts Brackets, clips, caps, covers, spacers, gears, feet, and fittings you can’t find in a store, made from your sample, photos, or measurements.
  • Builder and remodel parts Discontinued cabinet, door, and furniture hardware, trim clips, end caps, and matching pieces for remodels and new builds. Non-structural only.
  • Hobby parts Model, RC, garden, woodworking, and restoration parts, plus jigs and fixtures for your bench.
  • Metal send-out We model; a job shop or caster makes metal. No home metal printer. Ask if your job needs metal.
  • Shop batches Once a part fits, repair shops, builders, and makers can order labeled small batches.
How to order: Use the quote form or call (318) 506-2279. Tell us who you are (homeowner, hobbyist, builder, or shop), what the part goes on, and send photos or measurements. We reply with fit-sample steps and pricing — this site is a quote desk, not a full public cart yet.
