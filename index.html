Who we serve

Many kinds of customers. One parts desk.

If you need a part you can’t buy, you can request it, whether it’s for your house, your hobby, a job site, or a customer’s repair. We are not a gift shop and not a race-to-the-bottom Etsy catalog.

1

Homeowners & individuals

Something in your house, garage, or kitchen is missing a knob, clip, cover, or small part nobody sells anymore. Send photos, measurements, or the broken original, and we’ll work out a fit sample with you.

2

Hobbyists & makers

Models, RC, garden, woodworking, collecting, restoration. If your project needs a custom piece, bracket, jig, or replacement part, we can make it.

3

Home builders & remodelers

Discontinued cabinet and door hardware, trim clips, caps, spacers, and matching pieces for remodels and new builds. Non-structural parts only.

4

Repair shops

You’re on the bench and need repeatable “fits [model]” parts for customer machines. Same quality bar, shop-friendly labeling, and batch options once fit is proven.

Starting line

If you can describe it, we can probably make it

Discontinued parts, custom fittings, one-off brackets, replacement knobs, jigs, and small batches. We copy or design from your sample, photos, or measurements, and label replacements as fits [model], never with fake factory branding.

A

Fit before volume

We confirm the fit with you on a sample before making more, so the part is right the first time it counts.

B

Plastic print · metal send-out

Functional polymer parts from our desk. True metal work goes to a job shop or caster — we model, they make metal.
